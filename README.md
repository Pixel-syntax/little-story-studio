## ✨ How It Works

1. ✍️ **Write a scene:** describe what happens in your story
2. 👕 **Style the character:** the app picks outfits that fit the story, and you can change them
3. 🏞️ **Choose a place:** flower garden, woodland, living room, star bedroom and more
4. 🎙️ **Add a voice:** narration and sound for the scene
5. 🎞️ **Export:** a short animated video you can keep

## 🧱 Built With

Vanilla JavaScript · Cloudflare Workers · a custom story engine · a puppet and rigged character studio · a scene and sound system
